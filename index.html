I am a Google Developer Expert (GDE) in Google Cloud Platform and a Senior DevOps Engineer at Lloyds Banking Group. My focus spans modern DevOps methodologies, site reliability engineering (SRE), Kubernetes orchestration, and cutting-edge autonomous AI agents.

With the advent of Agentic AI, I have been actively designing, deploying, and speaking about productionizing AI agents using Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). I enjoy unraveling multi-cloud complexities like Cloud Spanner Omni on GKE and creating self-healing cloud operations.

As a tech speaker and author, I regularly speak at international tech conferences, Google Developer Groups (GDG), Cloud Community Days, and publish in-depth engineering breakdowns on Medium.
