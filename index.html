I am a Google Developer Expert (GDE) in Google Cloud Platform and a Sr. Enterprise AI Engineer at Motive (previously Senior DevOps Engineer at Lloyds Banking Group). My focus spans production Agentic AI, modern DevOps, site reliability engineering (SRE), and Kubernetes orchestration.

With the rise of Agentic AI, I actively design, evaluate, and deploy production AI agents using Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK), WebMCP (browser-native tool contracts), LangGraph, vLLM on Cloud Run, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP)—alongside multi-cloud architectures like Cloud Spanner Omni on GKE.

As a Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), international tech speaker, and author, I have delivered 100+ sessions across Google I/O Connect, DevFest, Cloud Community Days, SREday, and AgentCon, and publish engineering deep dives on Medium.
